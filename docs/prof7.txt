Professor Martinez teaches Software Engineering.

Students appreciate Professor Martinez's emphasis on teamwork and real-world software development practices. The course includes several group projects that simulate professional environments and require collaboration among students. Reviews frequently mention that these projects are both challenging and valuable.

Many students praise Martinez for providing detailed feedback and encouraging continuous improvement throughout the semester. Lectures often include discussions about industry trends and practical software engineering techniques. Some students mention that grading can vary depending on the teaching assistant assigned to their project group.

The workload is moderate to heavy, particularly during major project deadlines. Despite this, students generally feel they gain useful experience that prepares them for internships and future careers. Most reviewers recommend the course because of its practical focus and hands-on learning opportunities.

Overall Rating: Positive
