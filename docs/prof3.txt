Professor Brown teaches Computer Architecture.

Students frequently mention that Professor Brown's exams are among the most difficult in the department. Reviews indicate that success requires consistent studying and a strong understanding of lecture material. Brown expects students to read textbook chapters before class and actively participate during discussions.

Although grading is considered strict, many students appreciate the organization and structure of the course. Assignments are detailed and designed to prepare students for exams. Some students feel overwhelmed by the workload, especially near major project deadlines. Others praise Brown for maintaining high academic standards and preparing students for more advanced courses.

Most reviewers agree that the class is not easy, but they acknowledge that they learned a great deal by the end of the semester. Students who are willing to put in extra effort often recommend the course despite its difficulty.
