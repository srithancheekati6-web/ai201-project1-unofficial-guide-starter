Professor Taylor teaches Database Systems.

Students generally have positive experiences in Professor Taylor's course. Reviews highlight clear lectures, organized course materials, and practical assignments involving SQL and database design. Many students appreciate that examples used in class closely resemble problems seen on homework and exams.

Taylor is known for responding quickly to emails and providing helpful feedback on assignments. Several students mention that office hours are productive and useful for clarifying difficult topics. Exams are considered fair and often easier than the homework assignments.

The workload is moderate, and students feel the course provides valuable technical skills that can be applied in internships and professional work. While some assignments can be lengthy, most students believe the learning experience is worthwhile and recommend the course to others interested in databases.

Overall Rating: Positive
