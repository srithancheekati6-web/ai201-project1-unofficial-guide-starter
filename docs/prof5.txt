Professor Wilson teaches Algorithms.

Students describe Professor Wilson as demanding but highly effective. Exams emphasize problem-solving and critical thinking rather than memorization. Many reviews mention that assignments can be difficult, but they help students develop a strong understanding of algorithm design and analysis.

Wilson provides detailed study guides before exams and encourages students to attend office hours. Several students note that the professor is patient and willing to explain concepts multiple times. While the workload is heavier than many other courses, students often feel the effort is worthwhile.

Reviews suggest that students who stay on top of assignments and practice regularly tend to succeed. Many former students credit Wilson's course with improving their analytical thinking and preparing them for technical interviews. The course is challenging, but it is also one of the most respected in the department.

Overall Rating: Positive
